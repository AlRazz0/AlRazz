# Identidad visible: El capo

La marca visible de la web es **El capo**. El pie de página muestra
**Muebles en melamina Marlon**. La cabecera, administración, mensajes de contacto,
descargas y metadatos utilizan el mismo nombre y el componente `Brand`.

El logotipo es una imagen PNG con transparencia generada con la herramienta
integrada de imágenes, a partir de la referencia cromada proporcionada por el
propietario. Las revisiones reducen el grosor de las letras y después inclinan
el conjunto, rodeándolo con un marco de puntas y curvas cromadas. Archivo:
`public/images/brand/el-capo-chrome.png`.

Las direcciones publicadas, identificadores internos y credenciales permanecen
estables. El nombre de la cuenta ya vinculada al autenticador puede seguir
apareciendo como AlRazz; el panel lo explica sin pedir volver a vincularla.

## Prompt de la revisión de grosor

> Use case: style-transfer / logo-brand. Refine the attached El capo logo as requested: make it MUCH FINER, THINNER and more elegant. Preserve exactly the words "El capo", their spelling and case, the gently italic horizontal arrangement, the polished silver/chrome material, the single four-point star to the right and the elliptical underline swoosh. Main correction: reduce actual letter stroke thickness by roughly 55 percent. Use slender elegant thin-weight sculpted typography with airy counters, narrow delicate stems and fine curves. Reduce the extrusion/bevel to a very shallow sophisticated chrome finish, with soft controlled silver reflections. Not a bold or inflated balloon logo. Star is smaller, needle-fine and delicate; swoosh is an almost hairline silver ellipse. Keep all letters clearly legible, no star replacing letters. Do not merely scale down the thick logo: redraw the lettering with thin strokes. Genuine transparent background; keep alpha, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no extra text. Wide compact layout, no cropping.

## Prompt final: inclinación y marco

> Use case: logo-brand / style-transfer. Refine the first attached El capo wordmark using the SECOND image as a reference for angular aggressive chrome ornament and diagonal energy. Keep exact readable text "El capo", uppercase E and all other letters lowercase, one space. Preserve the relatively thin elegant silver lettering of the first image; do NOT return to fat inflated lettering. Tilt the ENTIRE wordmark baseline diagonally upward from lower left to upper right about 14 degrees, not just italic letters. Surround the wordmark with a coherent flowing frame of sharp polished chrome tribal flourishes inspired by image two: long tapering spear-like tips, concave hooked curves, pointed star shards sweeping around the outer lower-left and upper-right perimeter. A bold aggressive cyber-chrome jewelry aesthetic, yet refined slender strokes. Ornaments should wrap AROUND the wording, never cross through or replace letters and never make it unreadable. Integrate the former star/ellipse into this new pointed flowing frame rather than stacking unrelated decorations. Reflections are polished silver, charcoal and white, with clean crisp bevels; no colored glow. Single complete composition with all sharp ends safely inside the frame, sufficient margin. Transparent background with real alpha, no black rectangle, no white rectangle, no checkerboard, no slogan or other text, no ®. Production-ready isolated brand logo for a website. Wide horizontal composition with moderate diagonal tilt, approximately 2.5:1 canvas.
